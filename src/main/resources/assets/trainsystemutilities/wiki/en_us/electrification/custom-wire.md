# Custom Wire Designs

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/wire-connector__custom__en_us.png)

Open the settings screen in the [Wire Connector](wire-connector.md)'s GUI mode and choose "Custom" to edit it. Turn the mouse wheel over a value, and the preview on the right follows it.

[[TOC]]

| Setting | Range | Step |
|---|---|---|
| Thickness | 0.01–0.30 | 0.01 |
| Tier Offset | 0–2.00 m | 0.05 m |
| Dropper Interval | 0.50–10.00 m | 0.25 m |
| 2 Rows | On / Off | Toggle |

A Tier Offset of 0 gives one tier, and any larger value gives two tiers. Values always stay between the lower and upper limits.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/wire-connector-preset-save__en_us.png)

With "Save Preset", type a name, then press Enter to save or Esc to cancel. A saved preset can then be chosen from the list. While a saved preset is selected its values are fixed, so go back to "Custom" to edit. Save a changed version as a new preset, and delete the ones you no longer need by right-clicking them.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/wire-connector-preset-delete__en_us.png)

Sag Mode is only for "Simple (single line)"; "Custom" is always made of straight lines. Wires are part of the world, so train presets do not include them. Wire design presets are saved on the Wire Connector item itself, up to 64 per tool.
