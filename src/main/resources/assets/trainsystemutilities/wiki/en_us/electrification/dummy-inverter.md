# Dummy Inverter

```embed:item id=trainsystemutilities:fe_inverter_dummy size=48 label=true
```

Decorative equipment with the same look and the same 3-block layout as the [FE Inverter](fe-inverter.md). It does not collect, store or use FE.

[[TOC]]

It lets you show pantographs being deployed and folded and touching the wire. The Electrification Detail of the Management Computer shows "(decorative mode)" for it. Right-clicking it with an empty hand shows no screen and no power information.

You can also carry it on a train together with a real FE Inverter; the real inverter then manages the power. Dummy Inverters alone store no FE.

It also works as decoration, such as an equipment box beside the track. Crafting one FE Inverter turns it into a Dummy Inverter, and crafting one Dummy Inverter turns it back into an FE Inverter. Its ID for getting one is `trainsystemutilities:fe_inverter_dummy`.
