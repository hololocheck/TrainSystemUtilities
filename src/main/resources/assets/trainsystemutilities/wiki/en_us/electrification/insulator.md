# Catenary Insulator

```embed:item id=trainsystemutilities:insulator size=48 label=true
```

The support point that wires attach to and that carries the power from the substation. Right-click the face you want to place it on: it stands up on a floor, hangs down from a ceiling and sticks out sideways from a wall. Wires attach to the insulator's tip.

[[TOC]]

Switch the [Wire Connector](wire-connector.md) to Placement Mode and right-click the start insulator and then the end insulator to connect them. One insulator can take several wires.

To feed power, connect the wire network to an insulator next to the [substation](substation.md). When the substation runs out of power, the wires connected to it lose power too. Breaking an insulator removes the wires connected to it.

Right-click it with the [Power Checker](power-checker.md) to see how many wires it has and whether they are energized.
