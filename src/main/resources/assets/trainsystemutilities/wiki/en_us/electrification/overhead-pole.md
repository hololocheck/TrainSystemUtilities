# Overhead Pole

```embed:item id=trainsystemutilities:overhead_pole size=48 label=true
```

A pole that holds up the wire on single-track sections. Right-click the top face of the ground or another block to place it. It faces one of 8 directions in 45° steps.

[[TOC]]

Right-click a standing pole from above to add another one on top, facing the same way. Once it is tall enough, place a [Catenary Insulator](insulator.md) on top of the pole. Wires are strung on insulators, not on poles.

On double-track sections, combine it with the [Overhead Truss](overhead-truss.md). For help placing poles to fit the track, see the [Overhead Pole Placement Helper](../tools/overhead-pole-auto-tool.md).
