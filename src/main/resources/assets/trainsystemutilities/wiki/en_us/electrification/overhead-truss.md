# Overhead Truss

```embed:item id=trainsystemutilities:overhead_truss size=48 label=true
```

A beam that spans several tracks and holds up the wires. It faces one of 8 directions in 45° steps, so it can also be placed on diagonal sections.

[[TOC]]

Right-click a placed truss to extend the beam from its end. You can also place it from the side of a diagonal [Overhead Pole](overhead-pole.md), and it follows the pole's direction.

Attach a [Catenary Insulator](insulator.md) above each track, and connect the insulators to each other with the [Wire Connector](wire-connector.md). Wires cannot be connected to the truss itself. The settings of the [placement helper](../tools/overhead-pole-auto-tool.md) also let you choose a layout that uses trusses.
