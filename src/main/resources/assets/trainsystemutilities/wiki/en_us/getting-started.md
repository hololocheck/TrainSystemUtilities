# Getting Started

For first-time TSU users, this page shows how to open the screens and the operations they share. You need a compatible version of Create installed. Check the download page for the exact required versions.

[[TOC]]

## Opening the screens

| What you use | How to open it / what it does |
|---|---|
| [Management Computer](management-computer/overview.md) | Place it and right-click it. Manages the route map, trains and timetables. |
| [Railway Management Block](railway-management.md) | Right-click a Create station block to register the station, then place it and right-click it. Sets up the station's train information and linked features. |
| [Poster Management Block](poster-management.md) | Place it and right-click it. Sets up images and slideshows. |
| [Ticket Vending Machine](structure/ticket-vending-machine.md) | Place it inside a station group and right-click it. Choose a destination and issue a ticket. |
| [Transit Terminal](tools/transit-terminal.md) | Hold it and right-click. Look up routes and timetables. |
| [Station Range Tool](tools/station-range-tool.md) | Choose a mode with Alt + mouse wheel, then select ranges or manage stations. |
| [Train Preset Tool](train-preset-tool/save.md) | Right-click in GUI mode. Browse, place and share consists. |
| [Wire Connector](electrification/wire-connector.md) | Connects insulators in Placement Mode. Right-click in GUI mode to open the settings screen. |
| [Overhead Pole Placement Helper](tools/overhead-pole-auto-tool.md) | Set it up in GUI mode, then place poles along the track in Select mode. |

On the Management Computer, switch between its features from the list at the top left. Buttons such as "Color" and "Layout" open their own settings screens.

## Common operations

Click buttons and switches. To change a number, point the cursor at the value and turn the mouse wheel. When a list is long, turn the wheel over the list to scroll it.

## Hints and F1 {#hints-and-f1}

Turn on "Hint" at the top right of a screen, and resting the cursor on an item for about a second shows its description at the bottom left. While the description shows, two seconds of work (moving the cursor, clicking, the wheel, keys) hides it; rest again and it comes back. F1 needs no waiting: it takes you to the wiki for the feature under the cursor. The Wiki button opens the guide to the whole screen.

## Public or private {#access-mode}

Switch between public and private from the face icon. When private, only the owner can open the screen (an OP cannot either). Make equipment that several people set up public, and equipment whose settings an administrator wants to keep private. Reading and linking with cards is handled separately from changing settings on the screen.

## Screen size

Screens follow Minecraft's GUI scale. In dialogs that support resizing, drag the grip at the bottom right to change the size.
