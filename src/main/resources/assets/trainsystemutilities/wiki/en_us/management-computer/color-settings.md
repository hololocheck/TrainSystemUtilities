# Management Computer Color Settings

```embed:color-settings
```

Change the colors of the text and route map on monitors. Open it from "Color" on the Management Computer.

[[TOC]]

Choose the element to change under "Target" at the top, then click a color below. You can adjust the title, border, train name, speed and status, destination, clock, stat values, signals, lines, stations, train icons and more.

Choose from 12 preset colors. "Reset" returns the selected element to its default color, and "Reset All" returns every element. The settings are applied to the monitors linked to the same Management Computer.
