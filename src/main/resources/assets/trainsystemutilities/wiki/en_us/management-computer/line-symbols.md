# Line Symbols Tab

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__symbol__en_us.png)

Create and edit the line symbols used in station information displays. Choose "Line Symbols" on the Management Computer.

[[TOC]]

"New" opens the [editor](symbol-editor.md). Left-click an existing symbol to edit it, or right-click it to bring up the delete confirmation. A symbol has two letters and a number, a border color and corner roundness.

Assign the symbols you made to stations from the [Stations Tab](stations.md). Deleting a symbol that is in use also removes its assignments to stations.

From 1.0.10 on, symbols and their assignments are saved to the [Memory Card](../tools/memory-card.md). While the card is out, the symbols on station monitors are hidden for the time being. Inserting the same card again brings them back.
