# Monitor Display

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/monitor/monitor-display__en_us.png)

*An example of a Railway Management Block's station monitor (the train stopped at the station, the next train to stop, line symbol, track number and time)*

You can show the Management Computer's information on linked monitors. Arrange the route map, the train list, the clock and more freely with the Layout Editor.

[[TOC]]

## Steps to display

1. Place the Management Computer and the monitors.
2. Register the target monitors with a [Monitor Link Card](../tools/monitor-link-card.md).
3. Put the registered card in the Management Computer's monitor slot.
4. The "Monitor" switch is on by default. If you have turned it off, click it to turn it on.
5. In "[Layout](layout-editor.md)", place panels and save. The layout is empty at first, and nothing is shown without panels.

Set the panels' positions and sizes with "[Layout](layout-editor.md)", and the color of each element with "[Color](color-settings.md)". If the card has several monitors registered, the Management Computer shows on the monitor registered first.
