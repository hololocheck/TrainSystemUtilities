# Management Computer Overview

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__map__en_us.png)

Equipment for checking information about the whole network and setting up trains, stations and monitors. Place the block and right-click it to open it. Register the track network with a [Memory Card](../tools/memory-card.md).

[[TOC]]

From the list at the top left, choose the "[My Route Map](route-map.md)", "[Trains](trains.md)", "[Electronic Schedule](schedule.md)", "[Stations](stations.md)", "[Line Symbols](line-symbols.md)" or "[Tickets](tickets.md)" screen. The "[Color](color-settings.md)" and "[Layout](layout-editor.md)" buttons at the bottom set how the monitors look.

Change a number with the mouse wheel over its value, and a switch by clicking it. Turn on "Hint" and press F1 to open the guide to the feature under the cursor.

## Public, private and saving the settings {#owner-face}

The face icon switches between [public and private](../getting-started.md#access-mode). Choose according to whether people edit together or only the person in charge makes changes. The Management Computer's settings can be saved to the Memory Card inserted in it. See "[Memory Card](../tools/memory-card.md)" for how.
