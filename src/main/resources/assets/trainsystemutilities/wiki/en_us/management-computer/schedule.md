# Electronic Schedule Tab

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__schedule__en_us.png)

Manage Create schedules as electronic schedules. You can edit them, export them to items and share them with other trains.

[[TOC]]

## Opening it and the kinds of schedule

Choose "Electronic Schedule" from the Management Computer's list and click a train.

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Electronic schedule | A schedule edited and shared on the Management Computer |
| Regular schedule | A schedule handed to the driver as an item. It cannot be edited here. |
| None | No schedule is set |

"Stop All" and "Resume All" at the top of the screen act on all trains of the same network at once. Stopping happens train by train, as each one arrives at a station.

## Setting the train type {#種別}

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__schedule-detail__en_us.png)

Point the cursor at "Type" in the details and choose with the mouse wheel: Not set, Local, Rapid, Sec. Rapid, Com. Rapid, Semi Exp., Express or Ltd. Exp. Monitors at stations and in trains show the same type. With "Not set", no badge is shown.

The type is set by hand. The automatic classification by number of stops used in older versions is not used.

## Editing {#sched-editor}

To edit, the train must be stopped and have a mob or Blaze Burner driver aboard. A schedule received through sharing is read-only. If you cannot edit, check the reason shown on the button.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__schedule-editor__en_us.png)

Press "Edit" to open the screen for setting instructions and wait conditions. From "Add Entry", choose Destination, Deliver, Fetch and so on, and set the stations you need. Reorder entries with the arrows and remove them with ×. Adjust the numbers of wait conditions with the mouse wheel.

Choose loop or one-way running and save with "Apply". "Cancel" discards the changes. Right-click a Blaze Burner driver using an electronic schedule to stop or resume its running.

## Exporting to an item {#export}

Open a train's details and put a blank schedule in the input slot of the export section, and it comes out as an item with that train's schedule written on it. With "Export All" on, all the schedules you put in are processed at once.

## Sharing with other trains {#share}

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__schedule-share__en_us.png)

Press "Share" in the details and turn on the trains to share with. Edits to the source are applied to the trains it is shared with. A train it is shared with stops when you turn it on, and again each time an edit is applied. A schedule received through sharing cannot be edited directly.
