# Stations Tab

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__stations__en_us.png)

A screen for checking station names, positions, the link with a Railway Management Block, line symbols and more. Choose "Stations" on the Management Computer.

[[TOC]]

Click a station's row to open its details. For a station with a linked Railway Management Block, you can choose which side the train doors open on: North, South, East, West, Both Sides or Closed. "Back" returns to the list.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer-station-assign__en_us.png)

To assign a line symbol, create the symbol first in the [Line Symbols Tab](line-symbols.md). Press the assign button on the station's row and choose a symbol from the list. Choosing "None" removes it. The symbol you set appears on station monitors, station name signs and other displays.

For managing a station group's members and name, or deleting it, see also the [Station Range Tool](../tools/station-range-tool.md).
