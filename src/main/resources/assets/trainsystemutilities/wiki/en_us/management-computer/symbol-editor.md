# Line Symbol Editor and Color Picker

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__symbol-create__en_us.png)

Edit a line symbol's letters, number, corner roundness and border color. Open it with "New" in the [Line Symbols Tab](line-symbols.md), or by left-clicking an existing symbol.

[[TOC]]

## Editing a symbol

"Letter 1" and "Letter 2" (A to Z) and "Number" (00 to 99) are changed by pointing the cursor at the field and turning the mouse wheel. "Radius" sets the corner roundness from 5 to 25 px; at 20 or more the symbol is a circle. Choose the "Border" color from 12 presets with the mouse wheel or by clicking. Check the result in the preview at the top. A symbol has black letters on white, and its border and divider line take the border color.

A new symbol starts as JA, 01, radius 12 px. "Save" adds it to the list; assign it from the [Stations Tab](stations.md).

## Choosing a color {#color-picker}

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/management-computer__color-picker__en_us.png)

Open it with the "Color Picker" button. Choose a color with the hue bar and the saturation/brightness panel. Click the Hex, RGB or HSL field to type a value, and press Enter to apply it. RGB values range from 0 to 255.

Check the color in the preview, then press "+ Add": it becomes the border color and is added to the list of custom colors. Click a custom color to use it, or right-click it to remove it from the list. The list is cleared when you close the Management Computer's screen. If you close without adding, the original color is kept.
