# Poster Management Block

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/poster-management__en_us.png)

Equipment that registers images from your PC and shows them on monitors as posters or a slideshow. Place it and right-click it to open the settings screen.

[[TOC]]

Choose PNG, JPG or JPEG images with "Pick File". Each file can be up to 5 MB, and one block can hold up to 64 images. Registered images are stored on the server.

Click an image's row to turn its display on or off, use the up and down buttons to change the order, and the delete button to unregister it. Scroll the list with the mouse wheel.

With "Fit to Monitor" on, the whole image fits on the screen with its aspect ratio kept. With it off, the image fills the display surface and the parts that stick out are cut off. Set the transition effect, interval and speed from "[Animation](poster-management/animation.md)". With only one image, it is shown still, without switching. To animate even a single image, turn on "Single Image Animation" (off by default).

Register the display targets with a [Monitor Link Card](tools/monitor-link-card.md) and put the card in the monitor slot. Display on monitors is on by default, and the images are shown on the registered monitors (if you have turned it off, turn it on with the switch). One management block can output to several monitors.
