# Creator Center

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/creator-center__main__en_us.png)

A screen for checking the response to your posts. Open your own [Profile](profile.md) from Preset Place and choose "Creator Center". It cannot be opened from other users' profiles.

[[TOC]]

You can check your number of posts, total downloads, total likes and followers, plus "Top Presets" and "Past 30 Days Analytics".
