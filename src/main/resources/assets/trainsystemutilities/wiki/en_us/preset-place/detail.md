# Preset Details

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/preset-place-detail__en_us.png)

A screen for checking a public work's 3D model, images, description and author. Click a work in the Preset Place list to open it.

[[TOC]]

"3D" opens an enlarged view: left-drag to rotate, Shift + left-drag to move, and use the mouse wheel to zoom. The zoom range is 0.2 to 8 times. Close it with Esc or by clicking the background.

"DL" saves it locally. After saving, you can place it from "My Presets". Click like again to remove it. Choose the author's name to open their [Profile](profile.md), where you can also follow or unfollow them.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/preset-place-detail-report__en_us.png)

If you find inappropriate content, open "Report", enter a reason of up to 500 characters and send it. An administrator reviews the content.
