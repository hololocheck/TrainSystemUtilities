# Preset Place Overview

```embed:item id=trainsystemutilities:train_preset_tool size=48 label=true
```

A sharing feature where you publish your own train presets and find and download other players' work. It signs in automatically with the Minecraft account you are playing with.

[[TOC]]

Hold the Train Preset Tool in GUI mode, right-click, and switch the dropdown at the top left to "Preset Place". Choose a work to see its 3D preview, images and description ([Preset Details](detail.md)).

You can like works you enjoy and follow their authors. Consists you download can be placed from "My Presets". Inappropriate posts can be reported, with a reason, from the details page.

To publish your own work, choose the upload icon of your preset in "My Presets" ([Uploading a Preset](upload.md)). Open your [Profile](profile.md) from your name or icon, and check the response to your posts in the [Creator Center](creator-center.md).
