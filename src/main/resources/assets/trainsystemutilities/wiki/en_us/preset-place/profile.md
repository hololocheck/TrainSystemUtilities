# Profile

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/preset-place-profile__en_us.png)

A page for checking a user's public works and posting activity. Open your own profile from your name and icon at the top of Preset Place, and other users' profiles from the author's name in [work details](detail.md).

[[TOC]]

On your own page, you can check your number of public works, total downloads, likes and followers, and open the [Creator Center](creator-center.md). On other users' pages, you can browse their public works and follow them.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/profile-icon-editor__main__en_us.png)

Editing the profile icon is available to supported official accounts. Load an SVG and set its size, position, background color and frame color. Regular accounts do not see the edit button.

If your sign-in has expired, it signs in again automatically when you open the screen.
