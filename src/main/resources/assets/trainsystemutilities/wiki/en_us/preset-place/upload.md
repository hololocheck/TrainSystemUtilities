# Uploading a Preset

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/preset-place-upload__en_us.png)

Send a consist you built and saved to Preset Place for publishing. Set the [browse screen](../train-preset-tool/browse.md) to "My Presets" and choose the preset's upload icon. Sign-in happens automatically when you attach images or publish.

[[TOC]]

Attach 1 to 5 images and enter a description of 1 to 4,096 characters. PNG and JPG images are supported. In the description you can use Markdown headings, lists, links, emphasis and quotes. Check how it looks in the preview.

When everything is filled in, "Publish" becomes available. Check the contents on the confirmation screen and send it. Ctrl + Enter also publishes. Your request is published after review.

The upload icon appears on presets you made yourself, where Preset Place is available. It does not appear on works downloaded from Preset Place.
