# Railway Management Block

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/railway-management__en_us.png)

Equipment that shows a station's train information and works with monitors, platform screen doors and announcements. First, hold the item and right-click a Create station block to register the station. Then place it like an ordinary block, and it links to that station.

[[TOC]]

Right-click it after placing to open its screen. Link it with a [Memory Card](tools/memory-card.md) to a [Management Computer](management-computer/overview.md), and the station's information can be used for operation management.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/railway-management__en_us.png)

The screen shows the train stopped at the station and the next trains to arrive. You can check the train name, number of cars, arrival and departure times, the assigned line symbol and more. The list of next trains turns its pages automatically.

From the monitor row, turn the display on or off and use "[Settings](railway-management/settings.md)" and "[Color](railway-management/color.md)". "Function" shows the platform screen door settings, and the announcement settings when SAS is installed. Change a number by turning the mouse wheel over its value.

To apply the same settings to all the monitors registered on this block's [Monitor Link Card](tools/monitor-link-card.md), use [Batch Apply](railway-management/settings.md#batch-apply). Choose [public or private](getting-started.md#access-mode) from the face icon, depending on whether people edit it together.
