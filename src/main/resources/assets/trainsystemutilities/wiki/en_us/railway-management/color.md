# Railway Management Block Color Settings

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/railway-management-color__en_us.png)

From "Color" on the [Railway Management Block](../railway-management.md), change the colors of the information display. Choose a "Target" and click a preset color.

[[TOC]]

You can change 10 items: arrival time, departure time, stop information, the line's service pattern, stop seconds, train name, next train name, section titles, countdown and track number. The line's service pattern means loop, shuttle and the like, and is separate from train types such as Local and Rapid.

12 preset colors are available. "Reset" returns the selected item to its default color, and "Reset All" returns every item. For double-sided monitors, the colors can be set for each face chosen with "Flip Side" in "Settings". Colors are set per Railway Management Block and are not covered by "Batch Apply".

This screen uses preset colors. For editing with a hue bar or HEX input, see the [color picker](../management-computer/symbol-editor.md#color-picker) of the Line Symbol Editor and others.
