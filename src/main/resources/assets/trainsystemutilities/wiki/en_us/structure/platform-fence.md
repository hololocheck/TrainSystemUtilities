# Platform Fence

```embed:items size=32 cols=3 label=true ids=trainsystemutilities:platform_fence_1m,trainsystemutilities:platform_fence_3m,trainsystemutilities:platform_fence_5m
```

A fence placed along the edge of a platform. Choose among three kinds, 1m, 3m and 5m, and change the color of its band to match the line.

[[TOC]]

Face the direction you want it to stand and right-click. The fence extends to your left and right. The 3m and 5m kinds cannot be placed unless the width they need is free.

To change the band color of several at once, register adjacent fences and platform screen doors as a group with a [Memory Card](../tools/memory-card.md). While you hold the card, the registered range is outlined. Shift + right-click removes the parts you don't need (the connected part comes off as a whole).

Put the registered card in the platform screen door settings of the [Railway Management Block](../railway-management.md), and set a condition whose action is "Color". Pick the color from the swatches on the settings screen; it changes on stop and depart events. Right-clicking a fence on its own does not change its color. For the detailed linking steps, see "[Platform Screen Door](platform-screen-door.md#link)".
