# Monitor Link Card

```embed:item id=trainsystemutilities:monitor_link_card size=48 label=true
```

A card only for registering where monitors display. Hold it and right-click a monitor to register the connected monitors as one group. Click the same group again to unregister it.

[[TOC]]

You can register several groups, and the tooltip shows how many monitor blocks are registered. Shift + right-click clears everything registered on the card.

To display, put the registered card in the monitor slot of a [Railway Management Block](../railway-management.md), a [Poster Management Block](../poster-management.md) or a [Management Computer](../management-computer/monitor.md). You don't need to right-click the source block with the card. The Management Computer uses the first group registered on the card as its display. If monitor display is off on the source, turn it on (it is on by default).

Besides a single monitor, displays made of monitors joined vertically and horizontally are supported. Train information, images and custom layouts can be shown across the whole display.
