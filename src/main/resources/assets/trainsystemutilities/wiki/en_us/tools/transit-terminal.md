# Transit Terminal

```embed:item id=trainsystemutilities:transit_terminal size=48 label=true
```

A handheld information terminal. Right-click to open the information screen at the bottom right of the screen. While it is open, operate it with the mouse; close it with Esc.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/transit-terminal__top__en_us.png)

[[TOC]]

## Searching for a route

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/transit-terminal__top__en_us.png)

On "Top", enter the departure and arrival stations and choose stations from the suggestions. The swap button exchanges the two stations. Once both are chosen, you can run the search.

Select a result to see departure and arrival times, track numbers, train names and more for each station. When there are several candidates, switch between them with the candidate tabs. From the details, you can turn on navigation and the route details HUD. Search history can be deleted one entry at a time or all at once.

## Other screens

- Schedule: check trains' timetables while filtering by train name.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/transit-terminal__schedule__en_us.png)

- Map: shows the route map. Drag to move and use the mouse wheel to zoom.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/transit-terminal__map__en_us.png)

- Settings: switch between 12- and 24-hour time, adjust the panel positions, and turn the details HUD on or off.

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/transit-terminal__settings__en_us.png)

With layout adjustment on, drag a heading to move its panel. "Reset positions" returns them to their initial positions.

The information uses [station groups](station-range-tool.md) and Create's train schedules, and line symbols come from the [Management Computer](../management-computer/overview.md). If a station cannot be found, check the station group registration.
