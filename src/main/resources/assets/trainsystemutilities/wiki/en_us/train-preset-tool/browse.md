# Browsing and Placing Presets

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/train-preset-browse-mine__en_us.png)

A screen for choosing a saved consist, checking the materials it needs and placing it. Hold the Train Preset Tool in GUI mode and right-click.

[[TOC]]

## Choosing a consist

Switch between "My Presets" and "Preset Place" with the dropdown at the top left. "My Presets" holds the consists you saved, and "Preset Place" the works published on [Preset Place](../preset-place/overview.md).

Select one of your own preset tiles to show its 3D preview: left-drag to rotate, right-drag to move, and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Selecting a Preset Place tile opens its details screen. Right-click one of your own preset tiles to bring up the delete confirmation. The upload icon on a preset you made lets you [request publishing](../preset-place/upload.md).

Search public works by name and sort them by "New", "Popular", "DL" or "Views". After downloading, use them from "My Presets".

## Preparing materials

Drag a tile to the right panel to see the materials it needs. Turn the mouse wheel over the source button to choose Chest or ME. To link a chest, hold the tool and Shift + middle-click it.

Refill the glue from "Refill" in the glue tank section ([Material Refill](refill.md)). "Dump" throws away everything in the tank.

## Placing in the world

Press "Proceed with these settings" and confirm, and the screen closes and placement mode starts. In placement mode, switch between "Origin", "Rotate" and "Place" with Alt + mouse wheel. In "Origin", right-click a track to set the origin. The direction is set automatically; if needed, turn Ctrl + mouse wheel in "Rotate" to correct it in 90-degree steps. Right-click in "Place" to place it, using up materials and glue (in creative, glue only).

When a retry is possible, middle-click to retry. Shift + middle-click cancels and returns to GUI mode.
