# Material Refill

![](bws:trainsystemutilities:wiki/screens/train-preset-browse__refill__en_us.png)

A screen for refilling the glue tank used to place trains. Choose "Refill" in the glue tank section of the [preset browse and place screen](browse.md).

[[TOC]]

Put slime balls or slime blocks in the input slot, and the tank refills automatically.

| Material | Amount added |
|---|---|
| 1 slime ball | 10 |
| 1 slime block | 90 |
| Tank capacity | 10,000 |

Material that would not fit in whole stays in the slot. When you close the screen, any material left goes back to your inventory. "Dump" throws away everything in the tank.

This screen refills glue. In survival, the blocks that make up the train are taken from the chest or ME network chosen on the placement screen.
