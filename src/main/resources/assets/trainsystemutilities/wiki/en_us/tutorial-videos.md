# Video Tutorials

Watch how to use TSU and Manta without leaving the game — the video cards below play right inside Minecraft (Manta's embedded video feature).

> [!NOTE]
> The video runtime (Chromium) comes inside the mod and is set up the first time a video plays; nothing is downloaded. If it is not available yet, the card shows a notice instead.
